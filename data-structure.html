Core Architectural Takeaway: Settlla's data model is intentionally non-custodial and mandate-centric. By storing the property manager's written authority on the Property Listing, Settlla allows the manager to legally execute contracts on behalf of absentee landlords without requiring landlords to adopt software. By pairing Payment Transaction splits with isolated Escrow Hold and Caution Deposit Ledger entries, the platform provides institutional trust without operating an unlicensed bank.
