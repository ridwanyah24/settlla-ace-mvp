Phase 1: Search & Free Inspection Scheduling Steps 1–4

1
Browse Verified Listings
Tenant

Hajara opens the Settlla web application on her smartphone or laptop.

  • Feed Display: Vetted residential properties in Barnawa & Malali with transparent fee metadata.
  • Full Price Transparency: Displays annual rent, caution deposit (10%), legal drafting fee, agency fee, and total all-in move-in price.
  • Decision: Hajara selects a 1-bedroom flat in Barnawa matching her ₦600k budget.
2
Select Inspection Window
Tenant

Hajara taps "Book Free Inspection" on the listing page.

  • Pre-Set Windows: The calendar presents only visiting windows pre-set by the manager (e.g., Tuesdays/Thursdays 2–5 PM, Saturdays 10 AM–3 PM).
  • Slot Selection: Hajara picks an available 30-minute slot (e.g., Saturday at 11:30 AM).
3
Register & Confirm Booking
Tenant System

Hajara enters her name, mobile number, and email address, then clicks "Confirm Inspection".

  • System Action: Locks the 30-minute slot, sends instant SMS/WhatsApp confirmation to Hajara with exact apartment directions and manager contact info.
  • Manager Alert: Sends an automated SMS alert to HB&A Partners.
  • Cost: ₦0 (Arbitrary inspection fee extortion is eliminated).
4
Attend Physical Walkthrough
Tenant Manager

On Saturday at 11:30 AM, Hajara arrives at the Barnawa property.

  • Physical Tour: HB&A Partners conducts the in-person apartment tour, showing water flow, electricity meter, and fixtures.
  • Outcome: Hajara verifies the physical space matches the photos and decides to rent.

Phase 2: Identity Verification & Lease Generation Steps 5–8

5
Initiate Application & Upload Credentials
Tenant

In her web dashboard, Hajara clicks "Proceed to Rent This Property".

  • Credentials: Uploads NIN / government ID photo and NYSC/employment posting letter to GTBank.
  • Context: Confirms move-in date within her 14-day window.
6
Dynamic Tenancy Agreement Auto-Generation
System

Settlla automatically generates a standardized, legally binding Nigerian residential tenancy agreement governed by Kaduna State tenancy laws.

  • Auto-Populated Fields: Tenant name/NIN, landlord name, 12-month tenure, fee breakdown, and covenants.
  • Legal Signatory Clause: Formally designates HB&A Partners as the authorized attorney-in-fact signing under their written landlord mandate.
7
Tenant Digital Signature
Tenant

Hajara reviews the lease on screen, types her name, and draws her signature on the digital pad.

  • Audit Trail: Signature is cryptographically hashed with timestamp and IP.
  • Dispatch: Routes the agreement immediately to the property manager.
8
Manager Counter-Signature Under Mandate
Manager

HB&A Partners receives an SMS alert, reviews Hajara's verified ID, and counter-signs digitally on behalf of the landlord under their existing written mandate.

  • Execution: Dual-signed legal contract is generated as a secure PDF.
  • Status: Property transitions to "Reserved — Awaiting Checkout".

Phase 3: Checkout & Automated 4-Way Payment Split Step 9

9
Make All-in-One Payment via Secure Split Gateway
Tenant System

Hajara reviews the final invoice (e.g., ₦650,000 total) and completes payment via Paystack/Monnify (Debit Card or Bank Transfer).

1. Legal Fee (5%) Disbursed directly into legal drafter's bank account.
2. Agency Fee (10%) Disbursed directly into HB&A Partners' bank account.
3. Caution Fee (10%) Locked in merchant reserve / PayRep custody vault for 12 months.
4. Annual Rent (75%) Held in Key-in-Door Escrow Protection until key handover.

Hajara receives her digital Move-In Pass. Landlord receives SMS alert: "Rent secured in escrow; clears upon move-in."

Phase 4: Key Handover & Escrow Clearance Steps 10–12

10
Move-In Day & Physical Key Collection
Tenant Manager

On move-in day, Hajara arrives with her luggage. HB&A Partners meets her at the apartment, conducts a final check, and hands over the physical door keys.

11
Escrow Payout Trigger & Settlement
Tenant System

Hajara tests the keys and unlocks the front door.

  • Primary Trigger: Hajara taps "Confirm Key Handover" in her Settlla dashboard. The held rent instantly disburses to the landlord's First Bank account.
  • Safety Timer Fallback: If unconfirmed after 24 hours post-move-in time and no dispute is filed, funds automatically clear to the landlord.
12
Tenancy Active & Caution Ringfenced
Outcome

Hajara is safely housed within her 14-day deadline. Her caution deposit remains securely locked in the ringfenced vault until lease end, protecting her savings from landlord misappropriation.

Exception Flow: What Happens if There Is an Issue?

⚠️ Safety Protocol: "Report a Problem"

If Hajara arrives on move-in day and encounters fraud or major misrepresentation (e.g., keys do not work, flat was leased to someone else, or major undisclosed damage):

  1. Tap "Report a Problem": Hajara taps the dispute button in her dashboard before the 24-hour countdown expires.
  2. Instant Escrow Freeze: The platform immediately freezes the escrow payout; zero funds transfer to the landlord.
  3. Rapid Investigation: Settlla operations contacts HB&A Partners within 2 hours to investigate.
  4. 100% Indemnity Refund: If confirmed fraud or breach of contract, 100% of Hajara's annual rent and caution deposit are refunded to her account under Settlla's Scam Indemnity Guarantee.
