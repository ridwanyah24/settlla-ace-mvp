Architecture Invariants Highlighted in Main Flow

  • Zero-Fee Inspection Locking: Prevents double-booking of property managers and eliminates the predatory ₦3k–₦5k inspection fee.
  • Mandate Representation: The property manager signs digitally as the legal attorney-in-fact, sparing offsite landlords from account creation or software friction.
  • Non-Custodial 4-Way Split: Sub-account splitting executes through licensed fintech payment gateways (Paystack/Monnify), separating agency/legal fees, parking caution in a ringfenced merchant reserve/PayRep, and holding rent in escrow.
  • Dual Release Trigger: Escrow is unlocked either synchronously via the tenant's "Confirm Key Handover" button, or through an automated 24-hour timeout cron job if unconfirmed and undisputed.

Exception Safety Guarantees

  • Instant Freeze Invariant: Submitting a dispute synchronously halts the escrow release and voids the 24-hour auto-release countdown timer.
  • Zero Agent Bypass: Roadside agents cannot pressure the tenant for side-payments because Settlla holds the entire move-in pool in institutional escrow.
  • 100% Money-Back Indemnity: If landlord breach or access denial is verified, the system executes an automated reversal of all annual rent and caution funds directly back to the tenant's bank account.
