Settlla Screen Specification

Eight essential screens for the MVP rental-closing journey.

1. Verified Listings

Purpose

Lets a tenant find a vetted apartment that matches her budget, location, and commute needs.

Elements

  1. Settlla logo and navigation/account entry.
  2. Location, budget, and property-type filters.
  3. Neighborhood and commute-context chips.
  4. Verified listing feed; each card contains a verified badge, property photo, apartment type/location, amenities, annual rent, caution deposit, management fee, legal fee, all-in move-in total, and availability status.

Interactions

  • Filters update the listing feed.
  • A listing card opens Listing Detail & Inspection Booking.
  • Account opens the tenant dashboard/sign-in entry point.

2. Listing Detail & Inspection Booking

Purpose

Lets a tenant inspect a verified apartment and reserve a free 30-minute physical-tour slot.

Elements

  1. Back control, verified badge, listing title, location, and image gallery.
  2. Amenities, property details, commute context, and full fee breakdown.
  3. “Book Free Inspection” button.
  4. Booking drawer: property summary, manager visiting windows, date selector, available 30-minute slots, and selected-slot summary.
  5. “Continue” button.

Interactions

  • Gallery controls browse verified photos.
  • “Book Free Inspection” opens the booking drawer.
  • Selecting a date/window reveals valid slots; selecting one enables “Continue.”
  • “Continue” opens Inspection Registration & Confirmation.

3. Inspection Registration & Confirmation

Purpose

Lets a tenant provide contact details and confirm a ₦0 inspection booking.

Elements

  1. Back control and booking progress indicator.
  2. Property, address/directions, manager contact, and chosen date/time summary.
  3. Full-name, mobile-number, and email fields.
  4. “Confirm Inspection — ₦0” button.
  5. Confirmation state with booking reference, tour details, and “View My Booking.”

Interactions

  • Validation enables confirmation after valid details are entered.
  • Confirmation locks the slot, sends tenant and manager notifications, and shows the confirmation state.
  • “View My Booking” opens the dashboard; after touring, “Proceed to Rent This Property” opens Rental Application & Verification.

4. Rental Application & Verification

Purpose

Lets a tenant submit identity and supporting information required to prepare a lease.

Elements

  1. Back control, property summary, and progress indicator.
  2. Current residential-address fields.
  3. Emergency-contact fields.
  4. Government-ID/NIN-slip upload with status.
  5. Employment-proof/NYSC-letter upload with status.
  6. Consent checkbox.
  7. “Submit & Generate Agreement” button.

Interactions

  • Upload controls accept and preview documents.
  • Submit validates inputs, completes verification, generates the lease, and opens Lease Review & Tenant Signature.

5. Lease Review & Tenant Signature

Purpose

Lets a tenant read the auto-generated tenancy agreement and sign it digitally.

Elements

  1. Back control, agreement title, property summary, and status badge.
  2. Scrollable agreement viewer with parties, title reference, dates, fees, covenants, and mandate clause.
  3. Download/expand control.
  4. Legal-name field and signature pad with clear control.
  5. Acceptance checkbox.
  6. “Sign & Accept Agreement” button.
  7. Signed-pending-manager state with audit timestamp.

Interactions

  • Viewer controls let the tenant read or download the draft.
  • Signature pad records or clears a signature.
  • Signing timestamps and hashes the signature, notifies the manager, and shows pending status.
  • After counter-signature, checkout opens Secure Checkout & Move-In Pass.

6. Manager Lease Counter-Signature

Purpose

Lets an accredited manager verify tenant documents and execute the lease under the landlord mandate.

Elements

  1. Manager portal header and task indicator.
  2. Tenant, property, and agreement-status summary.
  3. Tenant ID and employment-document viewers.
  4. Landlord mandate viewer.
  5. Agreement viewer and signing audit trail.
  6. Manager signature control and authority-attestation checkbox.
  7. “Counter-Sign Under Mandate” button.
  8. Completion state with final-contract download and reserved status.

Interactions

  • Document viewers open credentials and mandate evidence.
  • Counter-signing applies the signature, creates the dual-signed PDF, changes the listing to “Reserved — Awaiting Checkout,” and notifies the tenant.

7. Secure Checkout & Move-In Pass

Purpose

Lets a tenant pay the all-in move-in amount through escrow and receive proof that key handover can proceed.

Elements

  1. Header with reserved-property summary and signed-lease download.
  2. Rent, caution, legal, management, and total-due breakdown.
  3. Escrow-protection explanation.
  4. “Pay Securely via Escrow” button.
  5. Gateway panel with card, transfer, and USSD options.
  6. Success state with receipt, Move-In Pass, move-in date, and “Go to Move-In Dashboard.”

Interactions

  • Pay opens the gateway; choosing a method begins its payment flow.
  • Successful payment splits fees, ringfences caution, locks rent in escrow, sends notifications, and shows the pass.
  • “Go to Move-In Dashboard” opens Move-In, Escrow & Tenancy Status.

8. Move-In, Escrow & Tenancy Status

Purpose

Lets a tenant confirm successful key handover or report a move-in problem before escrow is released.

Elements

  1. Property summary, Move-In Pass, lease download, and tenancy/escrow badge.
  2. Move-in instructions, manager contact, scheduled time, and 24-hour timer.
  3. Escrow summary and payment receipt.
  4. “Confirm Key Handover” button.
  5. “Report a Problem” button.
  6. Problem-report panel with category, description, photo upload, freeze notice, and submit button.
  7. Completion state with settlement confirmation, tenancy dates, and caution-vault notice.

Interactions

  • Key confirmation releases rent to the verified landlord and marks the transaction settled.
  • With no report, the safety timer releases rent 24 hours after scheduled move-in.
  • “Report a Problem” opens the form and pauses the timer.
  • Submitting a report freezes escrow, sends evidence to operations and the manager, and begins resolution or eligible rent-and-caution refund handling.
